## About Zoo Labs

Zoo Labs Foundation is a 501(c)(3) focused on:
- Decentralized AI (DeAI) research and experiments
- Decentralized Science (DeSci) initiatives
- ZIPs governance via [zips.zoo.ngo](https://zips.zoo.ngo)
- Community-driven research coordination

## Mission

Advance open, decentralized AI research for the benefit of humanity.
